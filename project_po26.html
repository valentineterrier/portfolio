ERACOM Open Day 2026

Poster for ERACOM Open Day, themed “Switzerland is Great!”.

The concept explores the contrast between the familiar environment of the school and iconic elements associated with Switzerland. Created as a photomontage, the poster transforms the corridors of ERACOM into an Alpine landscape, featuring a grassy field and an oversized wooden cow. This unexpected combination creates a playful and surreal atmosphere, blurring the line between reality and fiction.

The visual identity uses humour and surprise to capture attention and spark curiosity. While responding to the given theme, the poster offers an original and unconventional interpretation, inviting viewers to discover the event through a memorable and engaging visual experience.
