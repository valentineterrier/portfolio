Tulip Festival

Poster design for the Tulip Festival

The concept is inspired by the floral arrangements in the Parc de l’Indépendance, viewed from above. Tulips are simplified into colourful shapes, creating a dynamic and rhythmic composition.

A bright, spring-inspired palette reflects the festive atmosphere, while the typography creates a clear hierarchy and balanced layout.
