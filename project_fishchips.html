Fish & Chips

Brand identity for a fish and chips.

This branding project was created for a fish and chips food truck based in Lausanne, Switzerland. The visual identity is aimed at a young, urban and dynamic audience, including students and professionals looking for a homemade, high-quality meal served quickly in a friendly and relaxed atmosphere. The concept was designed for both lunchtime and evening service.

The project reinterprets the traditional image of fish and chips by presenting a lighter, crispier and more contemporary version of the dish. Inspired by the energy of Lausanne, the visual identity creates a modern, approachable and recognisable brand with a strong visual language that reflects freshness and quality.

The logo is built around an oval shape inspired by the silhouette of a fish, creating a clean, modern and dynamic identity. Careful microtypographic adjustments allow the letterforms to follow the oval shape, while two lines emphasise its outline and reinforce the overall balance of the logo.

The colour palette, centred around a pastel green, was chosen for its contemporary feel and its ability to communicate freshness. Within the minimalist visual identity, the colour becomes a key recognition element, making the food truck instantly identifiable.
