Fish & Chips

Brand identity for a fish and chips.

This branding project was created for a fish and chips food truck in Lausanne, Switzerland, targeting a young, urban audience of students and professionals. The identity was designed for both lunchtime and evening service, combining a homemade, high-quality offer with a friendly and contemporary feel.

The project reinterprets traditional fish and chips through a lighter, crispier and more modern visual identity inspired by Lausanne’s urban energy. The logo uses an oval shape inspired by a fish, with microtypographic adjustments following its form and two lines reinforcing its silhouette.

A pastel green palette brings freshness and serves as a strong recognition element, making the food truck instantly identifiable.
