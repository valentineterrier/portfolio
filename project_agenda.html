Agenda for ERACOM 2026-2027

Agenda designed for ERACOM students, takes a cultural and educational approach alongside its practical purpose.
At the beginning of each month, a different country is presented through a double-page spread, exploring the role and importance
of art in the country and highlighting a notable artist.

The project combines organisation, graphic design and cultural discovery, highlighting the importance and diversity of art around
the world. With the choice of a colorful color palette.
